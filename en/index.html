The Pré Mathy estate, between meadows and woods

At Pré Mathy, private tracks cross the meadows, run along the woods and climb to viewpoints you cannot see from the road.

Our Sur-Ron bikes are electric. There is no engine noise and no smell of petrol, so you can enjoy the landscape, the estate’s horses and the nature all around you.

A guide rides with you the whole way and adapts the pace to your group.

Good to know

A few simple rules let you ride safely on the estate.

Over 15 only

Rides are for people over 15, accompanied by a Pré Mathy Ride guide.

With a guide

For now, every ride must be done with a Pré Mathy Ride guide, at an extra €15.

Later, without a guide

When rides without a guide open, they will be reserved for holders of the required motorcycle licence who are at least 18. The same will apply to riding alongside someone on horseback.

Stay on site

The estate also offers accommodation. You will find all the details at premathy.be.
